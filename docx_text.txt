Core Business RulesTenant status remains PENDING until both the required advance and first-month rent are verified.Advance and first-month rent are separate records because advance is refundable while rent is not.Tenant ID and login credentials are generated only after successful initial enrollment payment verification.Room occupancy is automatic; a full room cannot be selected.Monthly rent is stored as ledger rows, never as separate month columns.Cash payments are manually confirmed by an administrator.Online payments are linked through a unique RAM payment reference and gateway transaction ID, not merely the common bank account.A verified online payment updates the ledger and creates a receipt.Unpaid rent can trigger recurring reminders after the configured due date; successful payment stops reminders.Vacate/refund is manual. Pending rent, damage, and other approved deductions can reduce the refundable advance.After vacating, the tenant record is retained for history and room availability increases automatically.16. Updated End-to-End Flow1. ADMIN: Create enrollment → details → proofs → room → advance + monthly rent.2. SYSTEM: Create separate ADVANCE and FIRST_MONTH_RENT payment records.3. PAYMENT: Verify initial collection.4. SYSTEM: Activate tenant → generate Tenant ID/login → update room occupancy → create first rent ledger.5. SYSTEM: Send welcome message with login details and separate advance/rent payment summary.6. MONTHLY: Rent ledger shows due date and amount to tenant.7. TENANT: Click Pay Now → payment request → gateway → verified callback → transaction → ledger update → receipt.8. CASH: Administrator confirms payment → ledger update → receipt.9. UNPAID: Automated due/overdue reminders continue according to policy.10. VACATE: Request → inspection → deductions → manual settlement approval → refund record → tenant VACATED → room space released.17. Recommended Build Order1. Database schema and migrations.2.
